New receipt — two copies printed per sale

Each transaction now prints two receipts back-to-back with a cut between them. They are identical except the footer: the Office Copy carries the signature line and the goods-received acknowledgement (kept by the shop, signed by the member); the Customer Copy drops both and is stamped “Customer Copy”. Green outlines mark the only differences.

Implementation plan — new template + two-copy print

The two copies share one body and differ only in the footer, so this is one templated component rendered twice, not two components.

1 · New template (BillContent)

  • Add a variant prop: "office" | "customer". Office renders the signature block + acknowledgement line; customer renders the “Customer Copy” label. Everything else is shared.
  • Rebuild the item table as 6 columns with two-line rows (index, name / unit-price·unit, qty·unit, discount, value). This is the part that needs width tuning for 72 mm — see the caveat.
  • New billData fields to add: member_name, installment_term, time on the timestamp, per-item uom.symbol + customer_discount (mostly wiring existing data), and shop address/phone from settings.

2 · Two-copy print loop (SalesView.printReceipt)

  1. Every sale prints both copies (cash and credit alike). Pick the printer path once (thermal vs system), as today.
  2. For variant in ["office","customer"]: set the variant → captureReceiptCanvas() → print that canvas. Each ESC/POS payload already ends with a cut, so the two copies separate cleanly.
  3. System/PDF path prints two jobs (or a 2-page PDF) the same way.

3 · Installment term captured at checkout

A new control in the checkout step lets the cashier pick the installment term (වාරික ගණන); the chosen value flows into billData.installment_term and onto both copies. The office copy keeps the signature block with no label; only the customer copy is stamped “Customer Copy”.

Confirmed decisions:

  1. Two copies on every sale — cash and credit both print office + customer.
  2. No “Office Copy” label — the office copy stays as your image shows; the signature block is the tell.
  3. Installment term is chosen at checkout by the cashier, per transaction.
